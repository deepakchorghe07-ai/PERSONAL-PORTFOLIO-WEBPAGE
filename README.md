*A Personal Portfolio Webpage is a website created by an individual to showcase their skills, projects, achievements, and professional information. It works like an online resume where developers, designers, students, and professionals present their work to others. The main purpose of a portfolio website is to help people demonstrate their abilities and make a good impression on employers, clients, or collaborators.

A personal portfolio webpage usually contains several important sections that give complete information about the individual. One of the main sections is the Home or Introduction section. This section gives a brief introduction about the person, including their name, profession, and a short description of what they do. It helps visitors quickly understand who the person is and what kind of work they specialize in*
